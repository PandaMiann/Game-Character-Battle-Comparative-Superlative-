📜 How to play

  1. Two teams play against each other. Every player has a hero card.
  2. Every hero has secret stats (numbers from 1 to 99): speed, strength, intelligence, creativity, luck, bravery and stealth (= being sneaky and hard to see). A higher number is better. You can't see the numbers - only the picture!
  3. Each round we announce a battle, e.g. "The battle of speed".
  4. Talk in your team in English! Who is the fastest? Choose your best hero.
    "I think Sonic is faster than Mario because..."
  5. Every hero can be used only once. Think carefully!
  6. FIGHT! The hero with the higher number wins +1 point.
  7. The losing team solves a grammar task. Correct answer = +0.5 point.
  8. 🚫 Speaking Czech = −0.5 point.
  9. After 7 battles, the team with the most points wins! 🏆
